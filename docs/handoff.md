## 1. The product, in one sentence

Put on a headset, stand inside any ordinary video, walk around while it replays exactly as it
happened. Aayan confirmed this wording tonight.

## 2. Decisions taken tonight (Aayan's, not ours to re-open)

- Strict ghost for the deadline: the viewer observes and touches nothing.
- The camera is a person at eye height walking on the floor, not a free camera. Walk mode is
  the demo default (`demo.html`; `?walk=0` gives the free camera back for authoring).
- Inventing content is fine as long as it looks decent. The world is generated by Marble and
  corrected against the footage; label what was never filmed, don't hide it.
- No live judge clips. Everything is pre-processed.
- Two moments to protect: "run alongside a character" and "stand where the camera never was".
- Three demo categories: a movie scene (Harry Potter playground candidate), a nostalgic clip
  (to be shot at the venue), a serious use case (incident review from bodycam/dashcam is the
  leading fit because it works in ghost mode plus possession).
- Thrown objects (bottles, balls) are in scope. Doors are out (a happy by-product if the labels
  give one).
- VR fallback is a live desktop demo. The reel (65 s, five sections) is the safety net.

Run in dev: `npx vite --port 5399 --host 127.0.0.1` (the `--host` matters: without it the
recorder cannot connect). `RECORD=1` disables hot reload so an agent saving `fourd.html` does
not kill a capture in progress. Never kill someone else's vite.

Pipeline: `python3 scripts/run_clip.py --clip public/clips/x.mp4 --name x --marble image
--all-people --fps 12`. It stops at a human gate before spending a Marble credit; look at the
cleaned frame it names, then re-run with `--gate-pass`. `--marble none` spends nothing.

## 4. How the pipeline works (one paragraph)

Cut check (a cut = a silent camera teleport). Then two lanes in parallel. World lane: erase
people, one clean frame to Marble, scale and place the world against depth measured from the
clip, fine-tune it where the camera looked. People lane: Pi3X solves the camera per frame,
MultiHMR tracks every person, an identity audit checks nobody swapped, LHM builds an avatar per
person and drives it per frame. Objects lane: a seedless detector finds every free flight of a
small object, fits a ballistic curve in world space anchored on the thrower's and catcher's
wrists, describes the object from its own in-flight crops, generates its shape. Package. The
viewer plays room, people and objects on the clip's clock.

## 6. Established negatives (do not redo; each cost hours; see share/ADVERSARIAL-LOG.md R1–R11)

- Refining body pose against the footage makes it worse (single-camera null space).
- Faces are unreadable below 64 px of face height; no per-clip head refit.
- Titanic "I'm flying" and both earlier Harry Potter clips fail reconstruction (no parallax or
  too dark). Only the wide dusk playground shot (95.92 s + 2.45 s) passes, and its world
  registration is being corrected tonight (R11).
- GEN3C as a static fill generator lost to the FLUX panorama fill (). Lyra 4D on real
  footage was never tried before tonight; result pending.
- Smoke, fire, water cannot be reconstructed from one moving camera.
- Every metre depends on assuming the subject is 1.70 m. Quote body-heights, not metres.
